## GPT-6.1 Terra: half price

On 26 October OpenAI launched GPT-6.1 Terra, a fast, efficient model in the GPT-6 family combining visual reasoning and computer use with clearer writing. The price is $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output tokens. OpenAI says that is half the price of GPT-5.6 Terra and GPT-6.1 Sol.

It is built for tasks such as reading screens, filling in forms and working through apps. According to Jisc, OpenAI's own system card rates it "Critical" for cybersecurity capability – a theme in this week's Deep Dives article.

## Gemini 4 Carbon: strongest, but locked

On 28 October Google introduced Gemini 4 Carbon, which it calls its strongest model with leading cybersecurity and long-horizon capabilities. Google says Carbon ranks first on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 and scores highly on benchmarks for finding, exploiting and patching vulnerabilities.

But access is limited. Google initially gives the model to a selected group of cyber defenders and expands gradually to trusted partners while refining guardrails. Google is also taking part in the US government's voluntary pre-release model testing. The price will match Argon: $2 input and $10 output per million tokens.

## Speech you can read before it is spoken

On 28 October NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, an open model for transcription, translation and speaker attribution in 40 languages that handles recordings up to an hour.

The same day Rime launched Prism, a speech-to-speech model for enterprise voice agents. What sets it apart is that it writes what it is going to say before saying it – so a company's rules can read every word before it becomes speech. Rime reports a response time of about 300 milliseconds.

## Also this week

On 27 October Google DeepMind showed Genie 4, a world model for interactive simulation with audio, to a limited group of testers. Microsoft AI launched MAI-Image-2.7 in preview for image editing. Liquid AI released LFM3, small open models for text, vision, audio and decisions on one shared backbone.

## FAQ

### Should we switch to GPT-6.1 Terra?

For high-volume tasks where a smaller model is enough, it is worth testing. A lower price per token does not always mean a lower price per task if the model uses more tokens or retries.

### When can we use Gemini 4 Carbon?

Google has not set a date. For now it is reserved for selected cyber defenders.

### Why does it matter that Prism writes the text first?

It makes it possible to check what a voice agent says before the customer hears it – for example that it does not promise discounts or give wrong information.
