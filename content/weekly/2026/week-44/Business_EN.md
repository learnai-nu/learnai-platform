## Cohere and Schwarz Group

On 29 October Canada's Cohere announced the initial close of a Series E targeting up to C$7 billion, about US$5 billion. The round is led by Schwarz Group, owner of Lidl and Kaufland, and investors include the Government of Canada and NVIDIA. The amount committed at the initial close was not disclosed.

Sources disagree on the valuation: Cohere states C$35.7 billion, while Bloomberg-based reports give US$20.5 billion. The gap likely reflects pre- versus post-money, but that is not confirmed.

Schwarz Group's participation builds on an earlier arrangement in which Cohere announced plans to acquire Germany's Aleph Alpha.

## Fractile: British chips for inference

UK-based Fractile raised about $720 million in a Series C at a $7 billion valuation, led by investors including Thrive Capital, Founders Fund and Lightspeed. Sources give the amount differently, between £540 and £577 million. It is a jump from a $220 million Series B at around $1.1 billion in May.

The jump follows a deal to supply AI inference chips to Anthropic. Fractile's chips are expected to be ready for data centres in 2027.

## What sovereign AI means

Sovereign AI means a country, region or company controls the models and infrastructure its AI runs on – rather than depending entirely on a few US vendors.

Cohere's round, led by a European retail group and a state investor, and Fractile's British chips point the same way: capital behind European and non-US AI is growing.

## What it means for you

**More vendors to choose from.** That brings negotiating power and the option to choose by data-location requirements.

**Ask about data location.** Where does the model run, and who can access the data? It is a legitimate procurement requirement.

**Be careful with big valuations.** The disagreement over Cohere's figures shows headlines are not always precise.

## FAQ

### What is sovereign AI?

Control over the models and infrastructure AI runs on, so you are not entirely dependent on a few foreign vendors.

### Is Cohere relevant for European companies?

Cohere focuses on enterprises and, with Schwarz Group, has a strong European partner. It may be an alternative where data location and vendor independence matter.

### Why do sources disagree on the figures?

An initial close means the final amount is not known, and valuations can be stated before or after the investment.
