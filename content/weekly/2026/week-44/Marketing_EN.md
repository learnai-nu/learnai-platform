## What AI actually reads

On 28 October Muck Rack published the fourth edition of its analysis of what AI assistants cite, based on more than 40 million AI responses and 207 million citations in 2026. Over 84% of citations came from earned and third-party sources that brands neither own nor pay for. Owned channels accounted for 15.2%, and paid content for just 0.3%.

The analysis also found journalism's share of citations fell from 27% in May to 22%, driven mainly by ChatGPT. And answers are unstable: when the same prompt ran twice in one day, ChatGPT repeated 41% of cited domains, Gemini 45% and Perplexity 94%.

For marketing, what others write about you weighs far more in AI answers than your own ads.

## The human has the final say

A survey by Alter Agents and Smartly, published on 27 October, found 77% of 500 marketers in the US and UK are comfortable with AI in advertising when a human has final approval. 99% already use AI professionally or personally.

The same day Sprinklr opened a beta of six marketing agents for ideas, creative, campaign execution, compliance, community and budget. Each delivers something a person must approve. The challenge: if agents speed up the process, more approvals land on the same people.

## Consumers are half ready

A study by the Electronic Transactions Association and TSG of 1,099 US consumers, published on 30 October, found 46% plan to use AI to compare prices this holiday season, while 23% would let an AI tool complete a purchase.

The same day Estée Lauder announced a unified data platform across its 24 brands and a Microsoft Copilot rollout to 14,000 employees.

## What to do now

**Prioritise earned coverage over ads for AI visibility.** Press, reviews and trade media are what AI reads.

**Measure your visibility repeatedly.** When answers change day to day, one measurement says little.

**Plan approval capacity.** If agents raise the pace, there must be enough people to approve.

## FAQ

### Can we buy visibility in AI answers?

According to Muck Rack, hardly: paid content is 0.3% of citations. Visibility comes mainly from coverage in independent sources.

### Why does it matter that answers change?

Because a single measurement can mislead. Measure over time and look at trends rather than single results.

### Should we let AI complete purchases in our webshop?

Only a minority of customers are ready. Make sure your product data is correct and structured so both people and AI can use it.
