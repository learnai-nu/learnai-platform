## The marketing platform moves into the assistant

On 13 October Movable Ink launched an MCP server exposing more than 50 APIs across its platform to external assistants, naming Claude and ChatGPT. A marketer can schedule and proof content, activate audiences, approve creative and pull reports without opening Movable Ink.

The same day CreatorIQ unveiled Q, an MCP-compatible agentic system for finding, recruiting and setting up creator campaigns within each client's approval rules. Full capabilities arrive in early 2027; available now are an analytics tool and a brand-safety feature that CreatorIQ says cut manual review from 40 minutes to about one minute per video.

The pattern is the same: work moves out of the vendor's interface and into the assistant the employee already uses.

## Agents get access to your data

On 12 October ZoomInfo launched a verified n8n integration, so an AI agent can look up a company, enrich a contact or check a buying signal partway through a workflow. It is ZoomInfo's third such connector since July, after Zapier and ClickUp.

The practical consequence: every workflow with access to your data vendor is another place where access must be managed and monitored. That is a shared job for marketing operations and IT security.

## Salesforce buys the explanation behind the numbers

On 12 October Salesforce signed a definitive agreement to acquire Loops, an AI analytics platform that monitors key metrics, explains why they change and suggests where to act. Loops can be triggered from Slack and email and will become part of Tableau. Terms were not disclosed.

The deal shows where analytics tools are heading: from dashboards someone has to check, to agents that report when something changes – and explain why.

## Customers still trust other customers most

On 13 October Bazaarvoice published a survey of more than 1,600 TikTok users in the US and UK. 96% say verified customer reviews make them more likely to buy, and 68% trust an everyday consumer more than an influencer they follow. As a purchase factor, influencer endorsement came last at 31%, behind discounts (75%) and star ratings and reviews (74%).

The same day a Liferay survey of 800 Americans found 56% have abandoned a purchase over poor localisation, and 82% believe they can spot machine-translated content. Worth remembering when agents write and translate campaigns at scale.

## What to do before Black Friday

**List assistant access.** Which AI accounts can reach your marketing platforms, and which actions may they perform?

**Write approval rules before switching agents on.** Which sends, price changes and creative need a named person?

**Budget human review of translated content.** Especially for multilingual campaigns.

**Load-test agent connections.** Bain expects more than $85 billion in US sales over the Black Friday weekend; new workflows should be tested before then.

## FAQ

### What is an MCP server?

The Model Context Protocol is a standard that lets AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT use other systems' functions directly. An MCP server from a marketing platform means the assistant can act in that platform on your behalf.

### Is it safe to let an assistant send campaigns?

It depends on how access is set up. Grant only the rights needed, and require human approval for sends, prices and promises to customers.

### Should we use fewer influencers?

The survey does not say influencers do not work, but that verified reviews weigh more in the purchase decision. Make sure reviews are visible where customers buy.
