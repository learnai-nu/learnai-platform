## Images and video get faster and cheaper

On 4 November OpenAI introduced Images 2.6 for image generation and editing in ChatGPT, Codex and the API, with faster generation and more consistent results across edits.

The next day Google made Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash generally available for generating and editing video. The model can produce clips up to 15 seconds, edit over several turns in a conversation and follow references more closely. Google puts the price at $0.07 per second in 720p – so a 15-second clip costs about a dollar.

## GPT-6 Cyber: approved users only

On 5 November OpenAI introduced GPT-6 Cyber, a model for finding and validating vulnerabilities within boundaries defined by security researchers. OpenAI says the model outperforms its predecessor and has already been used to find vulnerabilities and develop fixes. Access is limited.

It is the same pattern as Gemini 4 Carbon in week 44: the strongest security capabilities go to defenders first.

## New large models from Tencent and Thinking Machines

On 4 November Tencent announced Hy4, its latest large language model with image understanding, computer use and stronger long-running agent capabilities. According to Tencent, weights and system cards will be released in the coming days.

On 5 November Thinking Machines Lab presented Inkling 2 Small, a 594-billion-parameter model with image input. It is available through the company's Tinker platform, and the weights will only be released after external safety testing and red-teaming.

## Also this week

On 4 November Cognition introduced the coding model SWE-2 Flash in Devin. On 5 November Ant Group released Ling-3.2-flash, a multimodal model with a 1-million-token context window. Boson AI showed Higgs Realtime Duplex, a model that listens, sees and speaks at the same time.

## FAQ

### Is AI video ready for marketing?

For short clips and variants, yes. At about a dollar for a 15-second clip it is cheap to try. But always review the output for brand, rights and errors.

### Can we get access to GPT-6 Cyber?

Only as an approved security organisation. Ask your security provider whether they use it.

### Why is Thinking Machines holding back the weights?

So the model can be tested externally for misuse risks before it becomes available to everyone.
