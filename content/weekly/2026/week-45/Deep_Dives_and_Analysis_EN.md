## What Visa tested

On 5 November Visa published its Agentic Commerce Benchmark. Each model had to work through a conversation to uncover all of the customer's requirements and then make the right decision: buy or don't buy. 22 models from 12 labs were tested on 15 shopping tasks.

Overall, the models failed in 42.8% of test runs. And in 54% of the failed runs the agent still bought something – a wrong purchase, not just a missed one.

## Consistency is the number that matters

The best models passed 82.2% of attempts, the weakest 28.9%. But the number to plan around is a different one: when the same scenario was run three times, all three attempts passed in only 34.2% of cases.

So an agent that handles a task today will not necessarily handle it tomorrow. For a business that is decisive: testing an agent once is not enough.

## Consumers still want to approve

The same week, a Talkdesk survey of 1,048 US holiday shoppers found that 74% plan to use AI assistants for holiday shopping, but only 15% would let an agent use their payment details after a check. And in an FIS survey of 2,000 Americans, 50% said they want to approve every transaction an AI assistant makes.

The gap between asking an agent and letting it pay is wide – and Visa's numbers explain why.

## What it means for an online shop

**Make your product data unambiguous.** Agents fail most often when requirements and product information don't match. Precise specifications reduce wrong purchases.

**Review your returns policy.** If agents buy the wrong thing, the returns land with you. Consider how agent-made purchases should be handled.

**Build in approval.** Half of customers want to approve themselves. Make it easy to confirm a purchase before it goes through.

## FAQ

### Should we sell to AI agents?

Prepare, but don't expect large volumes yet. Only 15% would let an agent pay. Start by getting your product data ready.

### Who is responsible when an agent buys the wrong thing?

That is unresolved and depends on the terms of payment providers and agent platforms. Until it is settled, the return typically lands with the retailer.

### Why is consistency more important than the average?

Because an agent that only works every other time can't be trusted with payments. Test several times on the same tasks.
