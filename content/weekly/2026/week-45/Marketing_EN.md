## From report to campaign

On 3 November Supermetrics extended its integration with Claude from reporting to creating and editing campaigns on Google Ads, Meta, Microsoft Advertising, TikTok, LinkedIn and Snapchat. Every new campaign stays paused until a person approves it, and approval rules are set per account.

Supermetrics reports more than 6,000 customers on the Claude connector since February 2026. Until now, paid media reporting has been the dominant use.

## Adobe goes to the assistants

On 4 November Adobe placed its CX Enterprise Coworker inside ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Microsoft Copilot. Where other vendors have brought assistants into their own tools, Adobe goes the other way: out to the assistants employees already use.

The same week SeenThis brought video ad production into Claude and ChatGPT.

## Approval is the product

What the week's launches have in common is that what's being sold is not the AI's ability to build a campaign, but control over when it goes live. Supermetrics' pause feature is an example: the campaign is built automatically but isn't published without a person.

That fits Menlo Ventures' report from the same week, in which companies spent 2.5 times more on AI than budgeted, driven mainly by agents. As agents do more, controlling what they're allowed to do matters more.

## What you can do now

**Decide who approves.** Every connection between an AI assistant and an ad platform needs a named approver.

**Set limits per account.** Budgets, audiences and creatives can have different rules.

**Track spend.** When agents build campaigns, both ad spend and AI spend can grow faster than planned.

## FAQ

### Is it safe to let Claude create campaigns?

With a pause feature like Supermetrics', the risk is limited because nothing goes live without approval. Make sure the approver actually reviews the campaign.

### Should we use marketing tools through assistants rather than directly?

It depends on the workflow. For tasks that combine data from several tools, an assistant can save time. For detailed setup, the original tool is often better.

### How do we keep AI spend from running away?

Set budgets, monitor spend continuously, and review which agents run automatically.
