## Spending outruns the budget

On 3 November Menlo Ventures published its State of Enterprise AI 2026 report, based on a survey of nearly 400 US decision-makers responsible for more than $4 billion in AI spending. According to the report, enterprise AI spending reached $176.8 billion by September 2026, 5.4 times the $33 billion in 2025. The companies surveyed spent 2.5 times their budgeted amount.

The explanation is agents. On OpenRouter, which Menlo uses as one measurable channel, agents generated 80% of all tokens – about seven times human usage. Growth came mainly from companies already using AI that now use more. Menlo Ventures discloses that it has invested in Anthropic and OpenRouter, and that the figures are model-based estimates.

## Fal: billions to run generative AI

Fal, which provides infrastructure for running image, video and audio models, announced on 3 November a $1.3 billion Series F led by Bessemer Venture Partners, with NVIDIA among the investors. The valuation is about $16 billion, double April's. According to Bloomberg, Fal had more than $800 million in annualised revenue earlier in 2026, and the company had to restrict customer usage over the summer because demand exceeded capacity.

## Value lags behind spending

Gartner's survey of 2,002 CIOs and technology leaders, cited in a press release on 6 November, found that only 13% say their organisation has gained significant or transformational value from AI. 31% have a rule for when an AI pilot should not move to production.

Together with Menlo's numbers, a picture emerges: spending is growing faster than both budgets and measurable value.

## What it means for you

**Budget for usage, not licences.** Agents are typically billed per use, and the bill grows with the work.

**Introduce a stop rule.** Decide in advance what a pilot must show to continue.

**Measure before and after.** Without a baseline from before launch, value is impossible to document.

## FAQ

### Why are companies spending more on AI than planned?

Because agents work autonomously and consume many tokens. Budgets set per user don't fit spending that grows with the work.

### Is it a sign that AI doesn't work?

No, but it shows that measurement and governance are lagging. Only a few companies can document significant value yet.

### What is a stop rule?

A predefined threshold for when an AI project should be stopped or not moved to production because it doesn't deliver enough value.
