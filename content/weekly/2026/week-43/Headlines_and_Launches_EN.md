## Kimi K3.1 Pro: open and very large

On 23 October Moonshot AI launched Kimi K3.1 Pro, the first model in its new K3.1 series. Moonshot calls it its most capable 3T-class open-weight model to date, with a 1M-token context window and improvements in coding, 3D creation, financial research and agentic tasks. It also released Kimi K3.1 Pro Highspeed, which Moonshot says is two to three times faster.

It is October's largest open model, after Muse Spark Open and Beam in week 42. The pace of open models remains high – but as last week, read the licence before commercial use.

## Voxtral 3: voice agents with EU hosting

On 20 October Mistral introduced Voxtral 3, a family of speech-to-text and text-to-speech models plus an SDK that combines listening, reasoning, tool calls and speech in one real-time pipeline. Mistral says the models support more than 50 languages and are available through its EU-hosted API or for self-deployment.

For European organisations the point is not only quality but location. A voice agent for customer service or booking often handles personal data, and EU hosting or self-deployment makes it easier to keep that data within bounds.

## Canva builds its own image model

On 22 October Canva launched Craft, its first image-generation model built for design work. It is available in Canva and through a public API, producing images up to 2K with planned composition and output that can be edited afterwards.

That last part is the point: an image where you can fix one element is more useful to a marketing team than a beautiful image that must be regenerated for every change.

## Decision models get two new entries

On 21 October Base Labs, Baseten's research team, released mammoth-1 (27B parameters) and elephant-1 (9B) as open source. According to Baseten, mammoth-1 ranks first among 208 decision models on the Jev Decision Index.

Decision models do not answer with text but with a probability for each possible answer – which team owns a request, whether an email is phishing, how urgent a case is. It is the second week running with new models in the category, explored in this week's Deep Dives article.

## Also this week

Inception Labs launched Mercury 3, a diffusion-based language model with a 1M-token context window, on 19 October. MiniMax launched M3.1 Flash for agents, coding and cybersecurity the same day. Recursive released Flint, an open model for coding agents, on 21 October. Block presented Dessa, a speech model trained directly on human conversation, as a research preview on 23 October.

## FAQ

### Is Kimi K3.1 Pro relevant for a European organisation?

Mostly as a sign that open models are closing in on closed ones. The model is very large and needs significant infrastructure to run yourselves. Read the licence, and consider whether data may be processed by a Chinese provider if you use Moonshot's API.

### What is the benefit of EU hosting for voice agents?

Data that often contains personal information is processed in the EU, which makes GDPR and internal policies easier to meet.

### Does Canva Craft replace other image models?

Not necessarily. Its strength is design work inside Canva where output stays editable. Compare it on your own tasks, such as a poster or product graphic.
